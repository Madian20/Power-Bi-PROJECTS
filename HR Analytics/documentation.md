## Overview
A comprehensive Power BI dashboard for human resources analytics and workforce management. This project provides key insights into employee data, compensation, headcount distribution, and hiring trends.

## Features
- **Employee Metrics**: Total employee count, median salary, and sick days tracking
- **Workforce Demographics**: Balance days, average age, and department count
- **Marital Status Distribution**: Breakdown of divorced, married, and single employees
- **Salary Analysis**: Salary visualization by employee groups (full-time vs part-time)
- **Headcount by Department**: Visual representation of staff levels across all departments with performance categories
- **Education Levels**: Pie chart showing employee distribution by education (Bachelor, Doctor, College, Masters)
- **Hiring Trends**: Historical hiring data by year and quarter from 2013 to 2018
- **Geographic Filters**: Filter data by location (Houston, Los Angeles, New York City, Seattle)
- **Gender Analytics**: Toggle between male and female employee data

## Data Insights
The dashboard tracks 1,766K total employees across 15 departments with a median salary of 95K and displays hiring patterns, salary distributions, and departmental headcount metrics to support HR decision-making.

## Installation
1. Download the Power BI file
2. Open with Power BI Desktop
3. Connect to your data source
4. Refresh the data and explore the dashboard
